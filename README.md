# Tilty Martinis

A balancing game in a 1930s cartoon bar. Martinis drop onto a tray, and you keep it level by leaning your body left and right in front of your webcam. Glasses that land on top of each other stick together, so you can build towers, but the tray is slippery: tilt too far and they slide off. Three martinis on the floor and the party's over. Raise your hand to order another round.

**Live:** https://nikolaneutiens.github.io/tilty-martinis/

Made with three.js (`WebGPURenderer`), Shadertoy shaders ported to WGSL, Rapier physics, ml5 body tracking and a baked Blender scene.

## Controls

| | Body (webcam) | Keyboard / mouse |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tilt the tray | lean left and right | ← → |
| Menus | | ↑ ↓ + space / enter, or click |
| Another round | raise your hand above your head | space |
| Lean sensitivity | | + / − |

Before every round you stand up straight for a moment; that posture counts as "level". Without a webcam the game skips this and you play with the arrow keys.

## Shaders

All three are ported from GLSL (Shadertoy) to WGSL and plugged into three.js node materials with `wgslFn`.

| Shader | Original | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bubbles! by Weyland, based on Bubbles by Inigo Quilez | https://www.shadertoy.com/view/4slGDr (original: https://www.shadertoy.com/view/4dl3zn) | the fizzing drink inside every martini, in black & white |
| Raymarch Edge Detection by HLorenzi | https://www.shadertoy.com/view/4sf3Dr | the idea behind the ink outlines of the cartoon pass: an edge wherever the depth jumps |
| CRT screen | https://www.shadertoy.com/view/MsB3W1 | the curved old TV with colour split and scanlines that the whole game is shown on |

The shaders respond to the player:

- every martini you drop wears the film down: more grain, scratches, dust and flicker, plus a jolt at the moment it falls (`src/shaders/cartoon`)
- the drink fizzes inside every glass you catch, and the camera shakes and zooms out as your tower grows

The image goes through three passes: the scene is rendered into a texture (with its depth), the cartoon film pass turns it into a black & white 1930s cartoon with ink outlines, and the CRT pass puts that on a curved screen. The score, lives and menus are drawn on a canvas in between, so they sit inside the old TV too.

## Also used

- [three.js](https://threejs.org/) 0.186 with `WebGPURenderer` and TSL
- [Rapier](https://rapier.rs/) (`@dimforge/rapier3d-compat`): every martini is a rigid body; glasses that land on each other become one body
- [ml5.js](https://ml5js.org/) BodyPose (MoveNet), running on TensorFlow.js' WebGPU backend: shoulder angle for the tilt, wrists above the head for "another round"
- [Blender](https://www.blender.org/): the pub, tray and martini glass are generated by `blender/build_pub.py`, with the pub's lighting baked into a texture
- Google Fonts: Mr Dafoe and Caveat

## Run it locally

```bash
npm install
npm run dev
```

Then open the URL Vite prints, in a browser with WebGPU (recent Chrome or Edge). The webcam needs `localhost` or HTTPS.

## Rebuild the Blender assets

```bash
/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender -b -P blender/build_pub.py
```

This builds the pub, bakes its lighting and exports `public/assets/pub.glb`, `baked.jpg` and `props.glb`. Add `-- --preview` for a quick test render, or `-- --props` to only re-export the tray and glass.

## Project structure

```
index.html
src/
  main.js         game loop, menus, camera, render passes
  physics.js      Rapier world: tray, glasses, stacking, losing glasses
  bodypose.js     ml5 body tracking: lean and raised hand
  hud.js          score, lives, menus and guide, drawn on a canvas
  storage.js      best score and sensitivity in localStorage
  shaders/
    bubbles/      the drink
    cartoon/      black & white cartoon film, outlines, wear
    crt/          curved CRT screen
public/assets/    pub.glb, baked.jpg, props.glb
blender/          build_pub.py and pub.blend
```
